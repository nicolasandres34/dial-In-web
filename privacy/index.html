Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

EnglishEspañol

Dial In ("the app") is a free journal for home baristas: it saves the dial-in recipe of each coffee, your gear and your latte art photos. The app is free and may display advertising. This policy explains what data is collected, why, who receives it and the choices you have.

The app is provided by Nicolas Seura ("we", "us"). Contact: ndigitalhr@gmail.com.

2. Advertising

The app may show ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To show and measure ads, AdMob may automatically collect from your device:

  • Device identifiers: the advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, Android Advertising ID on Android), if you allow it.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it (not precise GPS location).
  • Ad interaction data: which ads were shown and whether you tapped them.
  • Device and app information: device model, operating system, app version, and diagnostics related to ads.

Google uses this data to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and, only if you consent, to show personalized ads. See how Google uses data.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): before any personalized ads are shown, the app asks for your consent. You can change your choice at any time in the app's profile settings.
  • iPhone (App Tracking Transparency): if you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising ID is not shared and you only see non-personalized ads. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

4. Legal basis (GDPR)

  • Contract: to provide the app you signed up for (account, recipes, photos, gear).
  • Consent: for personalized advertising and access to the advertising ID. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interest: to show non-personalized ads that keep the app free, and to keep the service secure.

5. Security

Each account can only access its own data: this is enforced by the database itself (row-level security), not only by the app. Photos are stored in a private bucket and shown through temporary links that expire. All connections are encrypted (HTTPS).

6. How long we keep your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete account in the app: this permanently and immediately deletes your account, coffees, photos, gear, latte art and activity. Backups kept by our hosting provider are overwritten within 7 days. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies.

7. Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or a similar jurisdiction, you have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. You can view, edit and delete your data in the app, or contact us at ndigitalhr@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.

Política de privacidad

Última actualización: 30 de octubre de 2026

Dial In es un diario gratuito para home baristas: guarda la receta calibrada de cada café, tu equipo y tus fotos de latte art. La app es gratis y puede mostrar publicidad. Responsable: Nicolas Seura, contacto: ndigitalhr@gmail.com.

Datos que nos das

Email y contraseña (guardada cifrada) o tu cuenta de Google si entras con Google; nombre y foto de perfil (opcionales); tus cafés y recetas; fotos de bolsas y latte art (privadas, solo las ves tú); tu equipo y tu historial de actividad. Se guardan en Supabase, en la UE (Irlanda). El idioma se guarda solo en tu teléfono. No vendemos tus datos ni usamos tus recetas, notas o fotos para publicidad.

Publicidad

La app puede mostrar anuncios de Google AdMob. AdMob puede recopilar el identificador de publicidad del dispositivo (si lo permites), la dirección IP y la ubicación aproximada derivada de ella, datos de interacción con anuncios e información del dispositivo, para mostrar y medir anuncios, prevenir fraude y, solo con tu consentimiento, personalizarlos.

Tus opciones: en la UE, Reino Unido y Suiza la app te pide consentimiento antes de mostrar anuncios personalizados y puedes cambiarlo en el perfil. En iPhone puedes negar el rastreo (Ajustes → Privacidad y seguridad → Rastreo). En Android puedes restablecer o borrar tu ID de publicidad (Ajustes → Google → Anuncios). Si no das consentimiento, verás anuncios no personalizados.

Proveedores

Supabase (base de datos, inicio de sesión y fotos, en la UE) y Google (inicio de sesión con Google, correos de la cuenta y, si se muestran, anuncios).

Base legal (GDPR)

Contrato (darte el servicio), consentimiento (anuncios personalizados) e interés legítimo (anuncios no personalizados y seguridad).

Seguridad

Cada cuenta solo puede acceder a sus propios datos, y eso lo impone la base de datos. Las fotos están en un almacenamiento privado y se muestran con enlaces temporales. Todas las conexiones están cifradas (HTTPS).

Conservación y borrado

Guardamos tus datos mientras exista tu cuenta. Puedes eliminarla en Perfil → Eliminar cuenta: se borran de inmediato tu cuenta, cafés, fotos, equipo, latte art y actividad. Las copias de seguridad de nuestro proveedor se sobrescriben en un máximo de 7 días.

Tus derechos

Puedes acceder, corregir, exportar o borrar tus datos, oponerte al tratamiento y retirar tu consentimiento escribiendo a ndigitalhr@gmail.com, y reclamar ante tu autoridad de protección de datos.

Menores

La app no está dirigida a menores de 16 años.
