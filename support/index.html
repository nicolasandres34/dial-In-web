Support

Need help or found a bug? Write to ndigitalhr@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Frequently asked questions

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot your password?, enter your email and use the code we send you to create a new password.

How do I change my grinder or espresso machine?

Tap your machine or grinder at the top of My coffees, or go to Profile → Manage gear.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Delete account. Your account, coffees, photos, gear and latte art are deleted permanently and immediately.

Can other people see my coffees or photos?

No. Your coffees and photos are private and only visible to you.
